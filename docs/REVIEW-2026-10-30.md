# Design review — 2026-10-30

Fresh-eyes review of DESIGN.md, REQUIREMENTS.md, RULES.md against docs/research/*.md and the raw AcmeApp build artifacts (r26 xcresult JSON, slf26, headers.txt, inv26.txt, DerivedData). 38 findings: 2 blockers, 18 majors, 18 minors. Resolution status is tracked in DESIGN.md Appendix B.
